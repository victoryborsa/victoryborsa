## Video direction

- **palette system** — Sevgio brand from the user's site work: teal `#127A76` (logo), deep teal `#0C4F4C`, white text, warm sun `#F2C14E` as a tiny accent. Photos carry the color; text sits on a soft deep-teal gradient at the bottom.
- **type** — Bricolage Grotesque 800 display, Figtree 500/600 for small text (the website's fonts).
- **logo** — Sevgio house-and-heart mark + "Sevgio" wordmark, top-left on every photo scene, large on the end card.
- **motion grammar** — slow, peaceful: every photo drifts (slow horizontal pan + gentle push-in) for its whole scene; text lines fade-rise softly (`power2.out`, ~1s). Crossfades between scenes. No snappy moves.
- **audio** — original ambient piano + pad bed (`assets/bgm/sevgio-peaceful.mp3`, composed for this video), fades in and out.
- **negative list** — no invented prices, ratings, amenities or addresses; no watermarked stock (Adobe Stock image excluded).
- **keep-out** — all text above y ≈ 1600.
