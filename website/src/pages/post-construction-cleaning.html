What a post-construction clean covers

Every job is quoted for its size and condition, so tell us what was built or renovated.

Fine dust removal

Dusting every surface top to bottom, including ledges, trim, doors, vents and light fixtures.

Floors

Vacuuming and mopping hard floors, and vacuuming carpets to lift construction dust.

Kitchens and baths

Cabinets inside and out, counters, sinks, fixtures, tubs, showers and appliance surfaces.

Windows and glass

Interior glass, sills and tracks wiped free of dust and residue.

Light debris

Leftover small debris swept up and bagged. Heavy construction waste stays with the contractor.

Final walkthrough

Ready for move-in, a listing photo shoot, or handing the keys to your client.
