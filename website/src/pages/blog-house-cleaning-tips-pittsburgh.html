10 House Cleaning Tips Pittsburgh Homeowners Swear By

Jul 1, 2026 · 4 min read

Pittsburgh is a beautiful city — but between the pollen in spring, humidity in summer, leaf debris in fall, and road salt tracked in all winter, keeping your home clean is a year-round challenge. After cleaning hundreds of Pittsburgh homes, our team at Shine Pro has learned what actually works.

Here are 10 tips that make a real difference.

1. Clean Top to Bottom, Always

This sounds obvious, but it's the rule most people break. Dust your ceiling fans and shelves before you vacuum. Wipe counters before you mop. Gravity is your enemy — don't undo work you've already done.

Start at the highest point in each room and work your way down to the floor. Every time.

2. Use a Microfiber Cloth for Dust (Not a Feather Duster)

Feather dusters just move dust around. Microfiber cloths trap and hold it. For Pittsburgh homes — especially those near the rivers or older neighborhoods with more particulate in the air — this makes a noticeable difference in how long surfaces stay clean.

Dampen the cloth slightly for best results on hard surfaces.

3. Tackle the Bathroom Weekly, Not Monthly

Bathrooms are the fastest room to get out of control. A quick 10-minute wipe-down every week is far easier than a 45-minute scrub every month. Keep a small spray bottle of all-purpose cleaner under the sink so there's no excuse not to do it.

Focus on: toilet seat and rim, sink basin, faucet handles, and mirror.

4. Don't Forget the Kitchen Exhaust Fan Filter

This is the most overlooked spot in most Pittsburgh kitchens. The grease filter above your stove collects months of cooking residue and becomes a fire hazard. Remove it and soak it in hot soapy water every 4–6 weeks. It takes 10 minutes and makes a huge difference in air quality.

5. Use the Right Product for Each Surface

Using the wrong cleaner can damage surfaces or just not work. A few rules:

  • Granite/marble counters: Use a pH-neutral cleaner, never vinegar
  • Stainless steel appliances: Wipe with the grain using a microfiber cloth
  • Hardwood floors: Damp mop only — never soak them
  • Glass shower doors: White vinegar and water works great

6. Declutter Before You Clean

Cleaning around clutter takes twice as long and produces half the results. Before you start, spend 5 minutes putting things away. Clear counters, pick up floors, and put dishes in the sink. You'll clean faster and the results will look dramatically better.

7. Let Cleaners Sit and Work

Most people spray and immediately wipe. That's not how cleaning products work. Spray your bathroom cleaner, then go do something else for 5 minutes. Come back and the grime wipes off effortlessly. This is especially true for toilet bowls — let the cleaner sit for at least 5 minutes before scrubbing.

8. Clean Your Cleaning Tools

A dirty mop just spreads bacteria. A clogged vacuum loses suction. Wash your mop head after every use, clean your vacuum filter monthly, and replace sponges every 1–2 weeks. This is the step that separates homes that look clean from homes that actually are clean.

9. Set a Rotating Deep-Clean Schedule

You don't have to deep clean everything every week. Instead, rotate tasks:

  • Week 1: Baseboards and door frames
  • Week 2: Inside the oven and refrigerator
  • Week 3: Windows and window sills
  • Week 4: Under furniture and behind appliances

This keeps your home consistently clean without overwhelming any single cleaning session.

10. Know When to Call in the Pros

Some jobs are just better left to professionals — especially deep cleans before a big event, post-renovation cleanup, or move-in/move-out cleaning. A professional cleaning team has the equipment, products, and experience to get results that are genuinely difficult to achieve on your own.
