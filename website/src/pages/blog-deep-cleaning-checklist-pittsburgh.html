The Ultimate Deep Cleaning Checklist for Pittsburgh Homes

Jul 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Pittsburgh's four seasons are beautiful — but they're hard on your home. Between spring pollen, summer humidity, fall leaf debris, and winter road salt, a standard weekly clean only goes so far. A proper deep clean two to four times a year keeps your home genuinely healthy and guest-ready.

Here's the room-by-room checklist our Shine Pro teams use on every deep clean.

Kitchen

The kitchen accumulates grease, food residue, and bacteria faster than any other room. Don't skip these:

  • Inside the oven — remove racks, soak in hot soapy water, scrub oven walls with a degreaser
  • Refrigerator coils — vacuum dust from the back or bottom coils (improves efficiency too)
  • Inside the refrigerator — remove all shelves and drawers, wash with warm soapy water, wipe walls
  • Range hood filter — soak in hot water and dish soap for 15 minutes, scrub, rinse
  • Cabinet fronts and interiors — wipe down with an all-purpose cleaner, check for expired items
  • Backsplash grout — scrub with a stiff brush and baking soda paste
  • Under and behind appliances — pull out the fridge and stove, sweep and mop underneath
  • Sink drain — pour baking soda followed by white vinegar, flush with boiling water

Bathrooms

Bathrooms need the most attention during a deep clean. Focus on areas that standard cleaning misses:

  • Grout lines — scrub with a grout brush and oxygen bleach cleaner
  • Toilet base and behind the toilet — wipe down the entire exterior including the floor around the base
  • Showerhead — soak in white vinegar overnight to dissolve mineral deposits
  • Exhaust fan — remove the cover, vacuum dust from the fan blades and housing
  • Caulk lines — inspect for mold; re-caulk if needed
  • Medicine cabinet interior — wipe shelves, check expiration dates on medications
  • Under-sink cabinet — remove everything, wipe down, check for leaks

Bedrooms

  • Under the bed — vacuum thoroughly, including the bed frame and slats
  • Mattress — vacuum the surface, spot-treat stains, flip or rotate if applicable
  • Closet floors and shelves — remove items, vacuum, wipe shelves
  • Ceiling fan blades — use a pillowcase to trap dust while wiping each blade
  • Window sills and tracks — vacuum debris from tracks, wipe sills with a damp cloth
  • Baseboards — wipe down with a damp microfiber cloth
  • Light switches and outlet covers — wipe with a disinfectant wipe

Living Areas

  • Upholstered furniture — vacuum all cushions, under cushions, and along seams
  • Blinds and window treatments — wipe each slat or launder fabric curtains
  • Air vents and returns — vacuum grilles, wipe with a damp cloth
  • Bookshelves — remove books, dust shelves, wipe book spines
  • Electronics — dust TV screens with a microfiber cloth, clean remote controls
  • Fireplace surround — wipe down the mantel, clean the glass doors if applicable

Whole-Home Tasks

  • Light fixtures — remove globes and wash, wipe down pendant lights
  • Door frames and tops of doors — often missed, always dusty
  • Walls — spot-clean scuffs and fingerprints with a magic eraser
  • Baseboards throughout — a damp microfiber mop makes this fast
  • Window interiors — clean glass, tracks, and sills in every room
  • Trash cans — wash with hot soapy water, let dry completely

Pittsburgh-Specific Tips

Spring: Focus on windows and screens after pollen season. Wipe down all exterior-facing surfaces.

Summer: Check for humidity damage in basements and bathrooms. Run a dehumidifier and clean any mold spots immediately.

Fall: Clean gutters and check that exterior doors seal properly to keep leaf debris out. Deep clean carpets before the winter.

Winter: After road salt season, mop entryways and mud rooms thoroughly. Salt residue damages hardwood floors if left too long.
