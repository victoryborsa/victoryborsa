Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Shine Pro cleaning services in Pittsburgh.

Booking & Access

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
No — most clients provide a key or door code. We are fully insured and background-checked so you can trust us in your home.
How do I book a cleaning?
Fill out our free estimate form — it takes under 2 minutes and we'll send your custom quote the same day. You can also call or text (412) 447-8047, Mon–Sat, 8am–6pm.
Do I need a contract?
No. There are no long-term contracts — book a single clean or a recurring plan. No contracts, no surprises.
What's your cancellation policy?
Plans are flexible: you can reschedule with 24 hours' notice. Just call, text, or email us.

What We Do

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes — we bring all cleaning supplies and professional equipment. You do not need to provide anything.
What's the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?
Deep cleaning goes beyond surface-level maintenance. We clean inside cabinets, appliances, and tackle built-up grime in areas that standard cleaning does not cover. See the full checklist.
How long does a cleaning take?
It depends on the size of your home and the service. Most 1–2 bedroom Airbnb turnovers are done in under 2 hours, and a deep clean typically takes 4–8 hours. We'll give you an estimate with your quote.
Do you clean if I have pets?
Yes. We use eco-friendly, non-toxic products that are safe for children and pets. Just let us know about your pets when you book.

Trust & Safety

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?
Yes. Every cleaner passes a thorough background check before their first job, and we are fully insured and bonded.
What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?
If anything isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean it at no charge. See the Shine Pro Promise.

Pricing & Payment

How do I pay, and when am I charged?
After your cleaning you'll receive an invoice, which you can view and pay in My Invoices in your client account. Pricing is flat-rate — what you see is what you pay, with no hidden fees.
Do you offer discounts for recurring service?
Yes — weekly 15% off, biweekly 10% off, and monthly 5% off. The loyalty discount is applied automatically.

Service Area & Airbnb

What areas of Pittsburgh do you serve?
We cover Pittsburgh and surrounding areas within 20 miles of Downtown. See all service areas.
Do you clean Airbnb properties on same-day turnarounds?
Yes — that's our specialty. We build our schedule around your calendar and can handle tight same-day windows. Just let us know your checkout and check-in times when you book.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Call us or send a message and we'll get back to you quickly.
