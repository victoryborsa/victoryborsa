Common questions

When do I get my $25 credit?
Your $25 credit is applied after your referred friend completes and pays for their first cleaning. We'll reach out to confirm and apply it to your next booking.
Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?
No limit. Refer as many Pittsburgh hosts or homeowners as you like — you earn $25 for every one who completes their first clean.
Does my friend need to use a code?
They just need to mention your name when booking — on the booking form, by phone, or by email. We'll match it to your account.
Can I use my credit on any service?
Yes — the $25 credit applies to any Shine Pro service: STR turnover, standard, deep, move in/out, or recurring.
Do credits expire?
Credits are valid for 12 months from the date they're issued.
