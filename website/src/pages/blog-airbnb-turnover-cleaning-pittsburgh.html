Why Turnover Cleaning Is Different from Regular Cleaning

A standard house cleaning maintains a home that's already reasonably tidy. An Airbnb turnover is a full reset — you're taking a space that was just lived in by strangers and making it look like no one has ever been there.

That means: - Stripping and replacing all linens - Checking for and removing all guest belongings - Restocking supplies (toiletries, coffee, paper goods) - Inspecting for damage - Cleaning every surface a guest might touch

And you often have to do all of this in a 2–4 hour window between checkout and check-in.

The Complete Airbnb Turnover Checklist

Bedroom

- [ ] Strip all bedding and replace with fresh linens - [ ] Fluff and arrange pillows hotel-style - [ ] Dust all surfaces including nightstands and lamps - [ ] Vacuum or mop floors - [ ] Check under the bed for forgotten items - [ ] Wipe down mirrors - [ ] Empty trash and replace liner - [ ] Check closet for guest belongings

Bathroom

- [ ] Scrub toilet inside and out - [ ] Clean sink and faucet handles - [ ] Scrub shower/tub and glass doors - [ ] Replace towels with fresh, folded set - [ ] Restock toiletries (shampoo, conditioner, soap, toilet paper) - [ ] Clean mirror - [ ] Mop floor - [ ] Empty trash

Kitchen

- [ ] Wash all dishes, pots, and utensils - [ ] Wipe down all counters and backsplash - [ ] Clean stovetop and oven exterior - [ ] Wipe down microwave inside and out - [ ] Clean refrigerator — remove any food guests left behind - [ ] Restock coffee, tea, and basic supplies - [ ] Wipe down cabinet fronts - [ ] Mop floor - [ ] Empty trash and replace liner

Living Areas

- [ ] Fluff and arrange couch cushions - [ ] Dust all surfaces - [ ] Wipe down TV remote and light switches - [ ] Vacuum upholstery and rugs - [ ] Mop hard floors - [ ] Check for forgotten items

Final Walkthrough

- [ ] All lights working - [ ] Thermostat set to welcome temperature - [ ] Locks and keypad working - [ ] Welcome book and house manual in place - [ ] No personal items from previous guests

How to Handle Same-Day Turnovers

Same-day turnovers — where checkout is at 11 AM and check-in is at 3 PM — are the most stressful part of hosting. A few strategies that help:

Have a dedicated linen set for each bedroom. This lets you strip and replace in one motion without waiting for laundry.

Keep a fully stocked supply kit on-site. Running to the store mid-turnover kills your timeline. Keep backup toiletries, paper goods, and cleaning supplies in a dedicated closet.

Use a professional turnover service. This is the single biggest time-saver for active hosts. A professional team can turn over a 2-bedroom unit in under 2 hours, and they'll flag any damage or missing items before your next guest arrives.

What Guests Actually Notice

After cleaning hundreds of Pittsburgh Airbnbs, here's what guests mention most in reviews:

  1. Smell — A clean smell (not overpowering fragrance) signals cleanliness immediately
  2. Bathroom — Guests inspect bathrooms closely; any soap scum or hair is an instant negative
  3. Bedding — Crisp, fresh linens make a huge impression
  4. Kitchen — Dishes and appliances need to be spotless
  5. Floors — Visible dirt or pet hair on floors gets called out in reviews
